🚵 **North Texas MTB Trail Status**

Have you ever pulled up the Dorba app on a rainy weekend and seen a bunch of closure statuses and wondered if any other trails in the region were open? Too lazy to check multiple other Facebook groups and websites with trail statuses? Then I have a solution for you.

Check real-time open/closed status for 54 trails across the North Texas Region — all in one place.

👉 https://ntx.trailstatus.workers.dev

Trail statuses are pulled directly from Trailforks and update every 5 minutes. Trails are organized by region and sub-region so you can quickly find what's rideable near you. Filter by region, search by trail name, and each trail includes a direct link to its Trailforks page. Works on mobile or desktop.

Check it out. Share with friends. Make suggestions and report bugs in the comments. 🚵

Happy riding.
