Why Do Trails Close?

North Texas trails are built on clay soil that becomes extremely soft and sticky when wet. Riding on saturated trails cuts deep ruts that can take weeks or months to repair and cause lasting damage to the trail system. The mud can also gum up bike components.

Volunteer trail stewards and crews monitor conditions and report status to Trailforks.com, which this page syncs to every 5 minutes. Updates depend on volunteers doing this on their own time, so please be patient after conditions change.

Status Guide

Open

All clear — no trail issues to report.

Caution

Minor issue present. Often means watch for muddy areas. Check Trailforks, the trail org website, or community pages for details before heading out.

Closed

Trail is closed and should not be ridden.

These statuses reflect the trail system as a whole. Individual trails within a system may have their own statuses — check the Trailforks link for full details.
